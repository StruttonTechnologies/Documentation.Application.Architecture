# Chapter 1 — Applying Architecture at Scale

The previous parts defined the architecture and explained how its responsibilities, boundaries, contracts, and dependency rules work together as a complete system.

The next question is how that same architecture behaves as the size and complexity of the system change.

Architecture is often confused with deployment style. Monoliths, service-based systems, and microservices are frequently treated as fundamentally different architectural approaches.

They are not.

They are different ways of organizing and deploying systems that may still follow the same architectural principles.

## In this chapter, you will learn

- the difference between architecture and deployment
- what scaling means beyond increased users or data
- how architectural structure evolves as systems grow
- common misconceptions about monoliths, services, and distribution

By the end of this chapter, you will understand why changing the application style does not replace the architecture and why scale should reveal architectural needs rather than dictate architectural trends.
