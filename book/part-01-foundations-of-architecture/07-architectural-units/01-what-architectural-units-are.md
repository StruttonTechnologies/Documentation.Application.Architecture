# What Architectural Units Are

Architectural units are the components that represent and enforce the structure of a system.

While layers define areas of responsibility and boundaries separate those areas, architectural units are what make those definitions real. They are the elements that carry the architecture into the actual system.

An architectural unit represents a defined area of responsibility.

It acts as a container for behavior that belongs to that responsibility, while also enforcing the rules that prevent other responsibilities from leaking into it.

Architectural units are not just organizational.

They are structural.

They exist to ensure that the architecture is consistently applied, not just described.

Without architectural units, structure becomes theoretical.

With architectural units, structure becomes enforceable.
