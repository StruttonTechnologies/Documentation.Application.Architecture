# Why This Distinction Matters

Confusing architecture with deployment leads to poor decisions.

Teams may adopt more complex system styles expecting them to improve structure. In reality, they often introduce additional complexity without solving the underlying problems.

A poorly structured monolith does not become better when it is split into services.

It becomes a poorly structured distributed system.

Understanding what stays the same prevents this.

It ensures that architectural discipline is maintained regardless of how the system is organized or deployed.

Understanding what changes also matters.

It prepares the system for the additional complexity that comes with scale and distribution.

This distinction provides clarity.

It allows decisions to be made based on actual needs rather than assumptions or trends.
