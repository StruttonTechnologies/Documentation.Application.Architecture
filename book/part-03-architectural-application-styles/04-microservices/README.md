# Chapter 4 — Microservices

The previous chapter introduced stronger business boundaries through service-based organization.

The next step is understanding what changes when those boundaries become independently deployed services.

Microservices are often presented as a solution to architectural problems. In reality, they are a distributed application style that introduces specific benefits together with significant communication, consistency, and operational costs.

This chapter explains how the same architecture applies across service boundaries and what must be accepted when the system becomes distributed.

## In this chapter, you will learn

- what microservices are
- how the architectural model applies within and between services
- what independent deployment can provide
- what distribution costs the system must absorb

This chapter focuses on architectural structure and trade-offs. It does not explain technologies, infrastructure platforms, or implementation techniques.
