# Chapter 2 — Architectural Layers

In the previous chapter, architecture was defined as the structure of a system.

The next step is understanding what that structure is made of.

Architecture is not a single concept. It is composed of parts that work together to organize responsibility and control complexity. One of the most important of those parts is layers.

This chapter focuses on layers as a conceptual tool.

## In this chapter, you will learn

- what architectural layers are and how they organize responsibility  
- how layers differ from physical structure such as folders or projects  
- why layers are used to maintain clarity and control complexity  
- common ways layers break down in real systems  

This is not about defining specific layers in a particular system. It is about understanding the role layers play in architecture so they can be applied correctly later.
